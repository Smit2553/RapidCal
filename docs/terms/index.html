2. Description of the Software

RapidCal is a local-first desktop calendar application engineered in Rust (Tauri v2) and SolidJS. RapidCal enables users to view, search, create, modify, and synchronize calendar events stored locally in an on-device SQLite database and optionally synchronized with connected third-party calendar providers, including Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365.

RapidCal operates directly on your personal computer without an intermediary CodeStacx cloud backend.

5. Calendar Synchronization, RSVPs & User Responsibilities

RapidCal performs two-way synchronization with your connected calendar accounts when you create, edit, reschedule, RSVP to, or delete events, or when you use features such as Cross-Account Busy Blocking. You acknowledge and agree that:

  • Real Changes to Connected Calendars: Actions you take inside RapidCal—including creating or deleting events, changing event times, sending attendee RSVPs (Yes / Maybe / No), or creating [Busy] mirror blocks—are pushed to your connected Google or Microsoft calendar and may trigger invitations or updates to meeting organizers and attendees.
  • Offline Outbox Queue: When you edit events while offline, RapidCal queues those changes locally in its outbox (outbox_mutations) and automatically transmits them when network connectivity is restored.
  • Verification of Critical Events: While RapidCal is engineered for high reliability and comprehensive recurrence (RRULE) and timezone accuracy, you remain solely responsible for verifying mission-critical appointments, deadlines, flights, medical visits, and time-sensitive commitments.
