Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026 Last Updated: October 8, 2026 Domain: rapidcal.codestacx.com
Core Privacy Summary: Zero Cloud Servers

RapidCal is a 100% local-first desktop application developed by CodeStacx (rapidcal.codestacx.com). RapidCal does not operate any cloud backend, relay server, or remote database. Your OAuth credentials, calendar lists, and calendar events are transmitted directly between your personal computer and Google or Microsoft APIs over HTTPS and stored exclusively on your local device.

1. Overview & Local-First Design

This Privacy Policy explains how RapidCal ("RapidCal", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by CodeStacx, accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the RapidCal desktop application (for macOS, Windows, and Linux) or visit our website at https://rapidcal.codestacx.com.

Unlike traditional cloud-backed calendar clients that ingest a copy of your calendar onto proprietary servers, RapidCal executes entirely on your local device. When you connect a Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 account, your computer authenticates directly with Google or Microsoft via an RFC 8252 loopback OAuth 2.0 + PKCE flow (http://127.0.0.1). CodeStacx never receives, views, or stores your calendar data or OAuth tokens on any external server.

2. Information We Access (OAuth Scopes)

RapidCal only accesses user data after you explicitly initiate an OAuth 2.0 sign-in flow and grant consent through Google's or Microsoft's official authorization screen. Specifically, RapidCal requests the following scopes:

2.1 Google Account & Google Calendar Data

  • Basic Profile & Email (openid, email, profile via https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v2/userinfo): We access your email address, display name, and profile picture URL solely to display your connected account identity inside the RapidCal sidebar and account switcher.
  • Google Calendar Read/Write Access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar): We access your Google Calendar list (calendar IDs, titles, color swatches, timezones, and access roles) and calendar events (titles, descriptions, start/end times, timezones, recurrence rules [RRULE], locations, video conference join URLs such as Google Meet, attendee email addresses, and RSVP statuses).

2.2 Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 Data

  • Basic Profile (openid, profile, email, User.Read via https://graph.microsoft.com/v1.0/me): We access your display name and email address / user principal name to identify your connected Microsoft account in the application.
  • Calendar Read/Write & Offline Access (Calendars.ReadWrite, offline_access): We access your Outlook / Microsoft 365 calendars and calendar events to perform two-way synchronization and event management on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

All data retrieved from Google APIs and Microsoft Graph APIs is processed strictly locally on your computer to provide the user-facing features of the RapidCal desktop calendar:

  • Local Calendar Rendering & Offline Search: Indexing your calendars and events in a local SQLite database (with SQLite FTS5 full-text search) on your device so you can browse, search, and inspect your schedule offline with sub-millisecond latency.
  • Two-Way Event Synchronization: Performing incremental synchronization using Google Calendar's nextSyncToken and Microsoft Graph's @odata.deltaLink, and pushing events you create, edit, reschedule, or delete in RapidCal back to your connected calendar provider.
  • Meeting & RSVP Management: Allowing you to accept, tentatively accept, or decline meeting invitations (Yes / Maybe / No) and launch video conferencing links (Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Webex) with a single keyboard shortcut.
  • User-Configured Cross-Account Busy Blocking: When explicitly triggered by you, mirroring an event's time window as a redacted [Busy] block onto another calendar account you have connected to RapidCal so your availability stays accurate across work and personal calendars.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Compliance

Mandatory Google API Limited Use Affirmation

RapidCal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In strict accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements, RapidCal guarantees that:

  • No Third-Party Selling or Transfers: RapidCal never sells, rents, licenses, trades, or transfers your Google user data to any third party, data broker, or analytics platform.
  • No Advertising Use: RapidCal never uses Google user data for serving advertisements, personalized advertising, retargeting, or interest-based profiling.
  • No AI or Machine Learning Model Training: RapidCal never uses your Google Calendar events, descriptions, attendees, or profile information to train, fine-tune, or improve artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • No Human Access: Because all Google user data is stored exclusively on your local workstation and never transmitted to CodeStacx servers, no employee, contractor, or developer at CodeStacx can view or read your Google user data unless you explicitly share diagnostic details with us while requesting technical support, or where strictly required by applicable law.

5. Local Data Storage & Security Measures

RapidCal implements defense-in-depth security controls on your local machine to safeguard your calendar data and authentication credentials:

  • In-Transit Encryption (TLS): All network communication between the RapidCal desktop application and Google APIs (*.googleapis.com) or Microsoft Graph APIs (graph.microsoft.com, login.microsoftonline.com) is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS using rustls.
  • Native OS Keychain Storage: OAuth 2.0 access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in your operating system's native hardware-backed credential manager—macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or Linux Secret Service (libsecret)—under the application identifier dev.rapidcal.desktop.
  • AES-256-GCM Encrypted Credential Vault: For headless or offline Linux/desktop environments where an OS keyring daemon may be unavailable, token sets are encrypted at rest using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with a unique cryptographic nonce before being stored locally.
  • Local SQLite Database: Calendar metadata and event instances are stored locally within your OS user profile's application data directory.

6. Data Sharing & Cross-Account Sync

CodeStacx does not share your personal information with any third parties. Your data only leaves your device in the following user-initiated scenarios:

  • Direct Sync with Your Calendar Provider: When you create, modify, RSVP to, or delete an event in RapidCal, those changes are sent directly to the Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook account that owns that calendar.
  • Cross-Account Busy Blocking (Optional): If you explicitly use RapidCal's Busy Block feature to mirror an event from Account A (e.g., personal Google Calendar) to Account B (e.g., work Microsoft Outlook or secondary Google Calendar), RapidCal writes a redacted [Busy] block containing only the start and end timestamps (omitting private descriptions and attendee lists) to Account B.

7. Data Retention, Deletion & Revoking Access

Because all data resides on your computer, you have complete, immediate control over its retention and deletion at any time:

  • Disconnecting an Account in RapidCal: Open Settings → Accounts inside RapidCal and click Disconnect next to any linked Google or Microsoft account. RapidCal immediately deletes the stored OAuth access and refresh tokens from your OS Keychain and encrypted vault, and purges all calendars, events, FTS5 search indexes, and pending outbox mutations associated with that account from your local SQLite database.
  • Uninstalling the Application: Uninstalling RapidCal and removing its local application data folder permanently erases all local databases, preferences, and cached events from your machine.
  • Revoking OAuth Permissions from Google or Microsoft: You can independently revoke RapidCal's OAuth token access at any time from your provider's security dashboard:
    • Google Account Permissions: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
    • Microsoft Account Consent Management: https://account.live.com/consent/Manage

8. Website Privacy, Cookies & Children's Privacy

  • No Tracking Cookies: Our public website at rapidcal.codestacx.com is a static informational site. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or behavioral fingerprinting. Standard, short-lived web server access logs (such as IP address and browser user-agent) may be processed by our hosting infrastructure solely for DDoS mitigation and security reliability.
  • Children's Privacy: RapidCal is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
