Engineered for Speed, Precision & Privacy

Unlike Electron wrappers or cloud-proxied calendar apps that copy your schedule onto third-party servers, RapidCal runs natively on your machine and talks directly to Google and Microsoft APIs.

01

Offline-First SQLite WAL + FTS5

Events and recurring rules are indexed locally in SQLite with Write-Ahead Logging and FTS5 virtual tables. Search thousands of events in under 2ms and edit schedules offline with an optimistic outbox queue.

02

Direct Loopback OAuth2 + PKCE

Authentication uses a local loopback listener (127.0.0.1) with RFC 7636 PKCE (S256). Your OAuth tokens never pass through any CodeStacx server and are sealed in your OS Keychain + AES-256-GCM vault.

03

Google Calendar & Outlook Delta Sync

Incremental background synchronization powered by Google Calendar v3 nextSyncToken and Microsoft Graph @odata.deltaLink, complete with bi-directional RFC 5545 RRULE translation.

04

Cross-Account Busy Blocking

Automatically mirror personal events as redacted [Busy] blocks onto your work calendar across Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook so colleagues never double-book your time.

05

1-Click Video Meeting Join

Press Cmd/Ctrl + J from anywhere to immediately launch the active or upcoming Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, or Webex call without hunting through email invites.

06

Keyboard-First Command Palette

Press Cmd/Ctrl + K or C to create events with RapidCal's built-in Rust natural language parser (e.g., "Team Sync tomorrow 2pm to 2:45pm"), switch views, or jump to any date.

How RapidCal Handles Your Google & Microsoft Data

Full transparency into the permissions RapidCal requests and how your calendar information is protected on your device.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Commitment

RapidCal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. RapidCal operates zero external cloud servers: your calendar data is stored exclusively on your local computer, is never sold or shared with third parties, is never used for advertising, and is never used to train AI/ML models.
